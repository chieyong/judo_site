After the summer we kicked off the new season of the talent programme. This year **six partner clubs** take part, spread across Lindeweerd, Oudeveen, Wilgenhoek, Kraanburg and Brakeldijk.

## What's new?

- An extra Saturday morning group for judoka aged 8 to 10.
- Fixed progress reviews in November and March.
- A parents' evening at the start of the season.

Does your child enjoy judo and want more? Talk to the coach at your own club. They can nominate your child for an introductory session.
