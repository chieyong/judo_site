On the Wednesday of the autumn holiday we host a training camp day at Sporthal Oudeveen. All talent groups train together, with separate sessions per age group in the afternoon.

The programme includes technique training, randori, judo games and a short talk on nutrition and rest for young athletes.

Parents are welcome to come and watch at the end of the day.
